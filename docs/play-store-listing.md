## Full Description (4000 characters max)

Chrometria brings the analytical power of academic music theory — pitch-class set theory, symmetry groups, interval vectors, voice-leading geometry — out of the graduate seminar and into your hands.

Whether you're a student learning to hear symmetry in Webern, a composer building atonal chord progressions, a researcher exporting Forte numbers to LaTeX, or a teacher running a live analysis session with your class, Chrometria has a tool for you.

---

**ANALYZE ANYTHING**

The Classifier shows the symmetry group, Forte number, and interval vector for any set of notes you select. The Analyzer processes uploaded audio or MIDI files and maps harmonic structure across time. The Live Detection tool listens through your microphone and identifies pitch-class sets in real time. Compare two pieces side by side to surface structural relationships that aren't visible to the ear alone.

---

**EXPLORE THE FULL UNIVERSE OF PITCH-CLASS SETS**

The Atlas organizes all 224 set classes by symmetry group so you can browse the entire landscape of tonal possibility. The Search tool lets you query all 4096 pitch-class subsets by interval content, symmetry properties, cardinality, or Forte number. Interval Cycles renders geometric visualizations of interval cycles on a chromatic clock face.

---

**BUILD AND COMPOSE**

Progression Builder constructs chord progressions with voice-leading optimization. The Voice-Leading Graph visualizes minimal chord movements as a force-directed graph. The Timeline lets you drag and drop harmonic events into an evolution chart. Melody analyzes melodic contour using CSEG, COM matrices, and contour similarity measures. Tuning extends the toolkit beyond 12-TET to 19, 24, and 31-tone equal temperament and Bohlen-Pierce.

---

**TRAIN YOUR EAR AND MASTER THE MATERIAL**

Ear Training lets you listen to pitch-class sets and identify them by sound. The Quiz module uses the SM-2 spaced-repetition algorithm — the same system behind many language learning apps — to help you build lasting fluency with set classes. Practice scores your sing-along accuracy against melodic contours.

---

**FOR EDUCATORS**

Classroom mode supports real-time collaborative analysis sessions with students. The Assignments tool generates printable worksheet templates. Score Annotation lets you upload MusicXML files and annotate them directly with symmetry analysis.

---

**FOR RESEARCHERS**

The Research tier unlocks batch processing, full API access, and academic export to Lilypond, LaTeX, and BibTeX. API documentation is built into the app. The About (The Math) section gives a rigorous account of the mathematical foundations underlying every tool.
